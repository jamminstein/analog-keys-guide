7) Chain Patterns

1. Prepare
Make patterns 1 and 2 in the same bank.
2. Hold
Hold that BANK key.
3. Start the chain
Press and keep holding TRIG 1.
4. Add
While holding trig 1, press TRIG 2.
5. Release
The screen shows the chain.
6. Play
Press CHAIN MODE, then PLAY.
Clear
CHAIN MODE + PLAY.
