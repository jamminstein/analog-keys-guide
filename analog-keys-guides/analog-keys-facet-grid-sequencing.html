2) Build a Sequence

1. Choose
Select a spare pattern, then TRACK 1.
2. Enter grid
Press REC.
3. Add notes
Hold trigs 1, 5, 9, 13 and press keyboard keys.
4. Listen
Press PLAY.
5. Edit
Tap a trig to remove it. Add another elsewhere.
6. Change page
Use PAGE for steps 17-64.
7. Add motion
Hold one trig and turn FRQ.
