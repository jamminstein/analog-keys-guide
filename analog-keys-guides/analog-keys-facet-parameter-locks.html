5) Add Parameter Locks

1. Start
Play a sequence, then press REC.
2. Lock one step
Hold a lit trig and turn FRQ.
3. Lock another
Hold another trig and choose a different value.
4. Hear it
Each step now recalls its own filter value.
5. Try more
Lock pitch, resonance or amp settings.
6. Clear one lock
Hold the trig and press the locked knob.
