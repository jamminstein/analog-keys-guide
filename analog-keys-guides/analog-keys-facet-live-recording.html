3) Record Live

1. Choose
Select a pattern and a synth TRACK.
2. Record
Hold REC + press PLAY.
3. Play
Perform a short phrase on the keyboard.
4. Exit recording
Press PLAY. The loop keeps playing.
5. Fix it
Press REC for grid mode. Remove unwanted trigs.
6. Perform
Sweep FRQ and mute with FUNCTION + TRACK.
