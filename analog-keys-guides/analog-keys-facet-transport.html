1) Understand the Instrument

PROJECT
Your whole workspace: patterns, kits, songs and settings.
BANK
A folder of 16 patterns. There are 8 banks: A-H.
PATTERN
The sequence. Choose one with BANK + TRIG.
KIT
The four synth Sounds plus FX/CV settings. A pattern links to one kit.
TRACK
A lane. Tracks 1-4 are synths; 5 is FX; 6 is CV.
SOUND
The synth patch loaded on one synth track.
Try it
Press PLAY, then switch between TRACK 1-4 and play the keyboard.
