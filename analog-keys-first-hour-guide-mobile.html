Analog Keys: First Jam

Follow in order.

1 Play

  • Choose: BANK + TRIG 1-16.
  • Press PLAY.

2 Tweak

  • Select TRACK 1.
  • Press FILTERS.
  • Turn FRQ.

3 Make a Beat

  • Press REC.
  • Press trigs 1, 5, 9, 13.
  • Press PLAY.

4 Change One Step

  • Hold one TRIG.
  • Turn FRQ.
  • Release it and listen.

5 Record Live

  • Hold REC + press PLAY.
  • Play the keyboard.
  • Press PLAY to exit recording.

6 Save

  • YES/SAVE + PATTERN.
Play. Tweak. Save.
